# Why EDDIE Is Built This Way

These documents explain the thinking behind EDDIE — the problems it solves, the research that shaped it, and the decisions that made it what it is.

You don't need to read these to *use* EDDIE. But if you're curious why it has five phases, why it forces research before architecture, or why it won't let you skip a gate, the answers are here.
